Principal Investigator

Marcelo Gehara, Ph.D. — Assistant Professor of Ecology and Evolutionary Biology

Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences, Rutgers University-Newark

I investigate how speciation mechanisms work across the tree of life, integrating Population Genetics, Molecular Ecology and Historical Biogeography with Genomics and Bioinformatics tools.
