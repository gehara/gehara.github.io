Research

Speciation and Phylogeography

We study speciation mechanisms across diverse taxa, combining population genetics with phylogeographic approaches. Our work spans tropical and temperate systems, with a focus on amphibians and reptiles.

Comparative Phylogeography in the Dry Diagonal

We investigate adaptation, hybridization and reticulated evolution in contact zones across the South American Dry Diagonal, in collaboration with Adrian Garda, Frank Burbrink, and Eliana de Oliveira.

Computational Methods for Demographic Inference

We develop statistical and machine learning tools for inferring population history from genomic data. This includes coalescent simulation, ABC methods, and neural network emulators — implemented in our R package PipeMaster.

Cacti Evolution

We study the evolutionary history of cacti in the Espinhaco mountain range, in collaboration with Evandro Marsolla.

Host-Symbiont Coevolution

We investigate coevolutionary dynamics between Amazonian Toucans and their parasites, in collaboration with Lukas Musher and Jason Weckstein.
