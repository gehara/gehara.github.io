Teaching

Courses at Rutgers-Newark

Evolution (undergraduate; Fall 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025)

Integrates current research findings and real-world applications into foundational evolutionary biology. Interactive lectures with videos, documentaries, and in-class discussions. Enrollment has grown from 79 to 117 students over five years. SIRS teaching effectiveness: avg 4.62/5.0 across six sections.

Evolution of Humans (undergraduate, writing-intensive; Spring 2023, 2025)

Upper-level course incorporating book readings with in-class discussion, challenging students to think critically about human origins from a scientific standpoint.

Computational Ecology (graduate; Spring 2021, 2022, 2024)

R programming, population genetic simulations, statistical inference, and landscape genetics. Designed to train graduate students in the quantitative and programming skills essential for modern ecological and evolutionary research.
