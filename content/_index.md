## Gehara Lab

{{< figure class="avatar" src="/images/pipemaster_sticker.png" alt="Gehara Lab">}}

**Evolution and Biodiversity**

Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences,
[Rutgers University-Newark](https://www.newark.rutgers.edu/)

---

We investigate how speciation mechanisms work across the tree of life.
Our research combines field and laboratory work on organisms outside
traditional model systems, integrating Population Genetics, Molecular
Ecology and Historical Biogeography with Genomics and Bioinformatics tools.

We develop computational methods for demographic inference, including
coalescent simulation, Approximate Bayesian Computation (ABC), and
neural network-based parameter estimation for population genetic analysis.
