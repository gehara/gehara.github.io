## About Me

{{< figure class="avatar" src="/images/hero.jpg" alt="Marcelo Gehara">}}

I am an Associate Professor of Ecology and Evolutionary Biology in the
Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences at
[Rutgers University-Newark](https://www.newark.rutgers.edu/).

I investigate how speciation mechanisms work across the tree of life. My
research combines field and laboratory work on organisms outside traditional
model systems, integrating Population Genetics, Molecular Ecology and
Historical Biogeography with the use of Genomics and Bioinformatics tools.
