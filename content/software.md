## PipeMaster

<img src="/images/pipemaster_logo.png" alt="PipeMaster" style="float:left; margin-right:15px; margin-bottom:10px; width:150px;">

An R package to build and simulate coalescent models for population
genetic and demographic inference.

**Features:**
- Coalescent simulation via msABC and scrm backends
- ABC rejection and neural network (SML) inference
- Shiny GUI for model building and visualization
- Support for RADseq and whole-genome sequence data

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/gehara/PipeMaster)
